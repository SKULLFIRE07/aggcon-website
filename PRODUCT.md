# AGGCON

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: choose the best implementation and build it end to end locally. Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, custom CSS, and a local SQLite database. Self-hosted assets and fonts.

## Users
Infrastructure contractors, EPC procurement teams and project managers choosing rental machinery. Secondary visitors include investors, prospective employees and enterprise partners. Derived from AGGCON's published content and Ravi Sehrawat's benchmark brief.

## Product Purpose
Present AGGCON as a capable infrastructure equipment rental partner. Help a visitor discover machinery, understand project experience and submit a project-specific rental enquiry. The user's immediate outcome is a beautiful working website running locally for presentation to AGGCON.

## Positioning
AGGCON Equipments International Limited was incorporated in 2003. Its published fleet spans foundation, lifting, concrete, road construction, earthmoving and material handling equipment. Its services include skilled operators and maintenance support. The company describes nationwide operations.

## Operating Context
Rental selection depends on project site, machine type, project duration, quantities and operating requirements. Availability and pricing require confirmation by AGGCON. Local enquiries are stored on the development machine; no email is sent to AGGCON and no external ERP is connected.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Home, searchable and filterable fleet, machine detail routes, a persistent quote shortlist, server-validated quote submission, projects and project stories, industry solutions, company, investor resources, contact and a protected local enquiry dashboard. Public data is sourced from AGGCON; precise specifications are only used when published. The visual asset is illustrative concept imagery. Do not fabricate testimonials, live availability, prices, certifications, financial metrics or completed project roles.

## Brand Commitments
AGGCON name and corporate identity. Ravi's benchmark sites: unitedrentals.com, sunbeltrentals.com, boels.com. User delegated selection among the three presented directions and explicitly requested the most beautiful ambitious implementation. Selected recommendation: cinematic industrial with practical equipment discovery.

## Product Principles
Make equipment discovery and project enquiries quick. Show the precise role AGGCON played in projects. Keep official documents easy to find. Preserve technical accuracy. Make the website usable on a phone at a worksite.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable controls, labelled forms, visible focus, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support and readable contrast.
